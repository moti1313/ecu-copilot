Nine diagrams cover the example, system context, runtime, data flow, investigation sequence,
evidence limits, benchmark, dashboard workflow and research context. SVG and PNG versions are in
`diagrams/`. They describe a prototype with synthetic data, not production deployment. The E-204
example of 483 microamperes, a 450-microampere limit and T1 at 35 degrees Celsius is illustrative,
not a recorded benchmark observation. Result numbers and later consistency checks are documented
separately in [RESULTS.md](../RESULTS.md).
