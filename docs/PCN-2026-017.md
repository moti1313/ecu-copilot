# PCN-2026-017 – End of life of HB4821-QHTS28, successor HB4830-QHTS28

## 1. Summary

The supplier has announced the end of life (EOL) of the H-bridge motor driver **HB4821-QHTS28**, used in DCU-A1 (position U7) and DCU-B2 (position U7). The pin-compatible successor is **HB4830-QHTS28**.

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| PCN received | 2026-02-10 |
| Last-time-buy (LTB) order deadline | **2027-03-31** |
| Last shipment | 2027-09-30 |
| Internal target: HB4830 qualified in DCU-A1/B2 | 2026-12-15 |

## 2. Affected products

| Product | Position | Part number | Qty per unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| DCU-A1 | U7 | HB4821-QHTS28 | 1 |
| DCU-B2 | U7 | HB4821-QHTS28 | 1 |

Annual demand approx. 2.4 million pieces (both variants).

## 3. Reason for change
Supplier wafer technology migration (180 nm to 130 nm BCD process). The HB4830 is produced on the new process.

## 4. Differences HB4821 → HB4830

| Parameter | HB4821 | HB4830 | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Package / pinout | HTSSOP-28 | HTSSOP-28, identical pinout | None – drop-in on the PCB |
| RDS(on) typ. | 55 mΩ | 42 mΩ | Lower losses; no impact on EOL limits |
| Over-current threshold typ. | 7.5 A | 7.0 A | Stall detection slightly earlier; software calibration check required |
| Sleep current typ. at 25 °C | 18 µA | 11 µA | Total quiescent current approx. 7 µA lower; EOL limits unchanged |
| Wake-up time | ≤ 500 µs | ≤ 300 µs | None |
| Qualification | AEC-Q100 Grade 1 | AEC-Q100 Grade 1 | None |

## 5. Required actions

1. Component Engineering: release HB4830 as alternative part in the BOM of DCU-A1 and DCU-B2 (status: in progress).
2. Hardware development: confirm over-current calibration and run the EMC delta test (planned Q3 2026).
3. Software: verify the stall-detection threshold in the window lift anti-pinch function against the lower over-current threshold.
4. Purchasing: place the last-time-buy order for the bridge period before **2027-03-31**; estimated bridge quantity 1.1 million pieces.
5. Production: lot-level traceability of U7 is mandatory from the first mixed lot (see FMEA-DCU-B2 item 14).

## 6. Risk if not implemented
Supply interruption of both DCU variants from Q4 2027. Grey-market sourcing of HB4821 is not permitted (counterfeit risk, FMEA-DCU-B2 item 14).
